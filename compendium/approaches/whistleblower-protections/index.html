Holding AI companies to account

Constraining

Whistleblower protections for AI workers

Give AI-company staff legal protection and anonymous channels to report safety risks without retaliation or gagging contracts.

Open in the compendium

Whistleblower protections let people who work at AI companies report safety risks to regulators or the public without being fired, sued or stripped of their pay. They also ban contracts that gag staff from raising risks. Insiders often see dangers long before anyone outside, so protecting them is one of the few ways to learn what happens inside frontier labs.

How it works

Protection has three parts. First, a channel: a confidential, ideally anonymous way to report, either inside the company or to a regulator. Second, a shield: a ban on retaliation, with remedies such as reinstatement and back pay. Third, a rule that contracts such as non-disparagement or non-disclosure agreements cannot waive these rights. Ordinary whistleblower laws mostly protect reports of illegal acts. The AI-specific push is to also protect reports of serious risks that no law yet covers.

The issue became public in May 2024, after reporting that OpenAI's exit paperwork tied departing employees' vested equity to non-disparagement terms; OpenAI then said it would not cancel vested units and released former staff from those clauses. In June 2024, 13 current and former OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Anthropic staff published 'A Right to Warn about Advanced Artificial Intelligence', endorsed by Yoshua Bengio, Geoffrey Hinton and Stuart Russell. It asked companies not to enforce agreements that bar risk-related criticism, to provide a verifiably anonymous channel to the board, regulators and an independent body, and not to retaliate against staff who go public after other channels fail.

Where research stands

Protection is now partly law. California's SB 53, signed on 29 September 2025, bars frontier developers from retaliating against employees and contractors who report catastrophic-risk activity, and requires them to notify staff of their rights and run anonymous reporting channels. In the EU, Article 87 of the AI Act applies the EU Whistleblower Directive to reports of AI Act breaches; the European Commission opened an anonymous AI Act Whistleblower Tool in late November 2025, and the Directive's retaliation protection has covered AI Act reports since 2 August 2026. In the US Congress, the bipartisan AI Whistleblower Protection Act (S.1792), introduced by Senator Chuck Grassley on 15 May 2025, would protect reports to federal authorities and Congress and give remedies for retaliation. As of late September 2026 it had not passed; Grassley was seeking a fast-track vote before the midterm elections.

Inside companies, practice lags. The AI Whistleblower Initiative's 'Publish Your Policies' campaign rates six frontier developers and finds that only OpenAI has published its internal whistleblowing policy, and none publishes data on how well its system works. A support ecosystem has grown up around potential whistleblowers, including a legal defence fund run by Legal Advocates for Safe Science and Technology. The main unresolved issues are how far protection should extend to risks that are not illegal, and how to square disclosure with trade secrets and hazardous information.

Key results

  • Thirteen current and former frontier-lab employees asked for four commitments: no enforcement of anti-criticism agreements, an anonymous channel to the board and regulators, a culture of open criticism, and no retaliation for going public after other channels fail. Jacob Hilton, Daniel Kokotajlo, Ramana Kumar, Neel Nanda, William Saunders, Carroll Wainwright, Daniel Ziegler and six anonymous signatories, 2024
  • After press reports, OpenAI said in May 2024 it had not and would not cancel vested units and released former employees from non-disparagement clauses unless they were mutual. Engadget, 2024
  • California's SB 53 bars retaliation against employees and contractors who report catastrophic-risk activity and requires anonymous internal reporting channels. Future of Privacy Forum, 2025
  • Of six frontier developers rated, only OpenAI has published its internal whistleblowing policy, and none publishes outcome data on its system. AI Whistleblower Initiative, 2026
  • The EU's AI Act Whistleblower Tool, launched in late November 2025, lets people professionally connected to AI providers report anonymously; Whistleblower Directive protection covers AI Act reports from 2 August 2026. eucrim, 2025

Open problems

  • Protecting reports of serious risks that are not yet illegal, which most whistleblower laws do not cover. source
  • Balancing disclosure of risks against trade secrets and companies' intellectual property. source
  • Showing whether internal reporting systems actually work, through published data on reports, outcomes and retaliation complaints. source

Criticisms

  • Ordinary whistleblower protections are insufficient because they focus on illegal activity, while many AI risks are not yet regulated. Signatories of A Right to Warn (2024)
  • Whistleblowing by AI-lab employees is likely to clash with intellectual property rights and trade secrets, so any regime must strike a balance with protecting innovative companies. Vincent Fauchoux, DDG Avocats (June 2024)

Who works on it

  • AI Whistleblower Initiative: Nonprofit supporting AI whistleblowers; runs the campaign rating whether frontier developers publish their whistleblowing policies. Publish Your Whistleblowing Policies (2026)
  • Legal Advocates for Safe Science and Technology: Runs a legal defence fund that pays legal costs for people exposing AI safety concerns. AI Safety Whistleblower Legal Defense Fund (2026)
  • Center for AI Policy: Published a report on whistleblower protections for AI employees with Psst.org and OAISIS/Third Opinion. Whistleblower Protections for AI Employees (2025)
  • Center for Humane Technology: Endorsed the federal AI Whistleblower Protection Act with 20 other organisations. CHT Supports The AI Whistleblower Protection Act (2025)
  • European Commission (AI Office): Operates the anonymous AI Act Whistleblower Tool for people connected to AI providers. AI Act Whistleblower Tool (2025)
  • US Senate Committee on the Judiciary (Chairman Chuck Grassley): Introduced the bipartisan AI Whistleblower Protection Act in May 2025. Grassley Introduces AI Whistleblower Protection Act (2025)

Status

Partly law. SB 53 bars retaliation against safety staff who report catastrophic risks. The federal bill, introduced 15 May 2025, would make its protections impossible to waive by contract.
