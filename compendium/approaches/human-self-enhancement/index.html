AI and people's own judgement

Societal resilience

Human self-enhancement and empowerment

Raise human capability directly, so humans can keep pace with AI rather than be replaced by it.

Open in the compendium

This approach tries to raise human capability directly, especially intelligence, so that people can keep pace with AI and stay able to oversee it, rather than being replaced by it. Supporters argue that smarter humans would be better at solving alignment and at governing a world with powerful AI. It is a long-range bet, and its methods, from brain-computer interfaces to genetic approaches, raise large scientific and ethical questions.

How it works

The idea is to improve the human side of the human-AI balance instead of only constraining the AI side. The Survival and Flourishing Fund (SFF) states the case plainly: "Human self-enhancement, especially intelligence enhancement, creates an alternative path for humans to keep pace with AI, both economically and ecologically." It points to past self-enhancements such as eyeglasses, antibiotics and vaccines as precedents.

Proposed routes vary widely in speed and risk. The writer Tsvi Benson-Tilsen (TsviBT) surveyed them in October 2024: brain emulation, genetic approaches in adults or before birth, signalling molecules, direct brain-to-brain or brain-computer interfaces, neural transplantation, and "support for thinking" through software and social tools. He judged genetic approaches before birth the most likely to work, which also makes them slow, since a child must grow up. The 2023 Shallow Review of alignment agendas grouped related ideas as human enhancement, merging human brains with AI systems, and using brain data to help align AI.

Where research stands

This is an early, small and contested field within AI safety. Funding is new: SFF's 2026 Human Self-Enhancement and Empowerment round offers $2-4 million for "technical research advancing human self-enhancement technologies" and "philosophical research on how they should be used". Applications closed on 8 July 2026 and recommendations are expected by the end of November 2026. Foresight Institute has funded neurotechnology, brain-computer interface and whole brain emulation work as an AI safety focus area since August 2023. The Berkeley Genomics Project, a two-person team that includes Benson-Tilsen, runs meetings and publishes material aimed at accelerating reproductive genetic technology, which it sees as the most feasible route to intelligence amplification.

No method has shown large gains in human intelligence. Even advocates expect strong methods to take many years; Benson-Tilsen judges that there is a "quite substantial chance" of enough time before AI risk arrives, which is a contested bet on AI timelines. A Foresight Institute workshop report described brain-computer interfaces and other in-body enhancement as an expensive, time-consuming and high-risk pathway to AI alignment.

Key results

  • A survey of strong intelligence-amplification methods calls genetic engineering before birth "the way that will work", sees adult brain gene editing as limited, and flags brain emulation as hard to test, dangerous if partly successful, and prone to value drift. TsviBT (Tsvi Benson-Tilsen), 2024
  • A report on a May 2024 workshop of 60 experts on brain emulation, brain-computer interfaces and neurotechnology for AI alignment describes brain-computer interfaces and other in-body enhancement as an expensive, time-consuming and high-risk pathway, and prosocial AI approaches as a lower-cost, faster option. Foresight Institute, 2024
  • Using theory, simulations and real data, the study estimated that screening embryos on current polygenic scores would raise expected cognitive ability by only about 2.5 IQ points on average, with wide prediction intervals. Ehud Karavani, Or Zuk, Danny Zeevi, et al., 2019

Open problems

  • Find any enhancement method that gives large gains fast enough to matter before advanced AI arrives. source
  • Work out how enhancement technologies should be used and governed; SFF funds philosophical research on this alongside technical work. source
  • Show whether brain data or brain interfaces can help align AI, not just augment people. source

Criticisms

  • Brain-computer interfaces and other in-body enhancement are an expensive, time-consuming and high-risk pathway to alignment. Foresight Institute (workshop report)
  • The genetic route depends on predicting traits from DNA, and current predictions give only small expected gains (about 2.5 IQ points from embryo screening) with wide uncertainty. Ehud Karavani and co-authors

In their own words

Artificial intelligence is a critical factor because if humanity isn't careful, AI could entirely replace the human species, and we don't want that. ... Human self-enhancement, especially intelligence enhancement, creates an alternative path for humans to keep pace with AI, both economically and ecologically, thereby empowering humanity to continue thriving and innovating for a better future.

Survival and Flourishing Fund, SFF-2026 Human Self-Enhancement and Empowerment (HSEE) Announcement, 2026

What empowers humanity is the ability of humans to notice, recognize, remember, correlate, ideate, tinker, explain, test, judge, communicate, interrogate, and design. To increase human empowerment, improve those abilities by improving their source: human brains. AGI is going to destroy the future's promise of massive humane value. To prevent that, create humans who can navigate the creation of AGI.

TsviBT (Tsvi Benson-Tilsen), Overview of strong human intelligence amplification methods, 2024
