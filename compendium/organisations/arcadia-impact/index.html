Arcadia Impact

London charity focused on reducing risks from advanced AI that runs the LASR Labs and AI Governance Taskforce research programmes and an alignment research team.

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Taking on unowned problems and agendas Building the field
    Take on safety problems and research agendas that no team owns, for example by founding a new organisation or lab.
  • Automated alignment research Data and training
    Use AI systems to do alignment research, so safety work can keep pace with growing capabilities.
  • Safety cases Testing before release
    A structured, auditable argument that a system is acceptably safe, borrowed from nuclear and aviation.
