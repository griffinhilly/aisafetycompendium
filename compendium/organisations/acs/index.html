Alignment of Complex Systems Research Group (ACS)

Prague research group studying systemic risks of advanced AI: gradual human disempowerment, LLM psychology, multi-agent AI ecosystems, and alignment targets based on how humans actually reason.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Multi-agent alignment and commitment dynamics Theory
    Prevent collusion, conflict or destabilising races between many AI systems.
  • Group agency and collective intentionality Theory
    Formal philosophy of when many agents constitute one agent.
  • Model introspection Looking inside
    Study whether models can accurately report on their own internal states.
  • Gradual disempowerment People's own judgement
    Study how humans could lose meaningful control step by step, with no single catastrophic event. One method simulates many small choices to rely on AI.
  • Indicators of human versus AI influence People's own judgement
    Build metrics that track how much of economic, cultural and political life humans still steer.
