Cooperative AI Foundation

UK charity that funds research on cooperation among AI systems and between AI and people, and publishes reports on risks from interacting AI agents.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work quickly through grantmakers and regrantors who know the field well enough to spot good projects.
  • Multi-agent alignment and commitment dynamics Theory
    Prevent collusion, conflict or destabilising races between many AI systems.
  • Multi-agent evaluations and collusion detection Testing before release
    Test groups of interacting AI agents for collusion, hidden signalling and miscoordination, and build monitors that catch it.
