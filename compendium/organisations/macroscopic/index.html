Macroscopic Ventures

A grantmaker and impact investor whose AI safety grants cover safety research, AI governance, cooperative AI and digital sentience (possible AI welfare).

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work quickly through grantmakers and regrantors who know the field well enough to spot good projects.
  • Multi-agent alignment and commitment dynamics Theory
    Prevent collusion, conflict or destabilising races between many AI systems.
  • Agreement verification technology Global coordination
    Build the technology and protocols that let governments, or AI developers, verify that an AI agreement is being kept.
  • Model welfare research AI welfare
    Investigate whether and when an AI system's welfare deserves moral consideration, and how developers should act while the answer is unknown.
