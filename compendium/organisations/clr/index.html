Center on Long-Term Risk

Research charity addressing worst-case risks from advanced AI, with work on multi-agent conflict and cooperation, bargaining, and how harmful traits emerge in language models.

Works on

  • Multi-agent alignment and commitment dynamics Theory
    Prevent collusion, conflict or destabilising races between many AI systems.
  • Reducing the motive to misuse Society and power
    Lower the number of people who want to cause mass harm, by fixing social causes such as inequality and poor mental health care.
