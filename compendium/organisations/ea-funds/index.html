EA Funds (Transformative AI Fund; Long-Term Future Fund, closed)

Runs the Transformative AI Fund, which makes early-stage grants, typically $10k to $150k, mainly to reduce catastrophic risks from advanced AI; it replaced the Long-Term Future Fund.
