PKU-Alignment (Peking University)

Peking University research team building open-source tools for language-model safety alignment: constrained RLHF, safe reinforcement learning libraries, safety datasets and deception benchmarks.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Learning from feedback (RLHF) Data and training
    Score the model on human or AI preference judgements so its behaviour tracks what raters wanted.
  • Constrained reinforcement learning and shielding Data and training
    Keep a learning agent inside safety limits. Either maximise reward subject to cost constraints, or add a shield outside the policy that blocks unsafe actions.
  • Sparse autoencoders and causal abstraction Looking inside
    Break a model's internals into parts a person can understand, using specific tools such as sparse autoencoders and causal abstraction.
  • Sandbagging and situational-awareness evaluations Testing before release
    Test whether a model hides capability or behaves differently when it knows it is being tested.
