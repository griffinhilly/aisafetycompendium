Longview Philanthropy

Philanthropic advisor whose Emerging Challenges Fund recommended grants against catastrophic risks from emerging technology, including advanced AI; the fund is closing after a final round in 2026.

Works on

  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work fast through people with taste and dealflow.
  • Public communication and content creation Building the field
    Explain AI risk to wide audiences through video, podcasts, books, social media and journalism.
  • Mechanistic interpretability Looking inside
    Reverse-engineer the network's internals into human-readable circuits and algorithms.
  • Agreement verification technology Global coordination
    Build the technology and protocols that let governments, or AI developers, verify that an AI agreement is being kept.
  • Concentration-of-power and lock-in research Society and power
    Study how AI lets a small group entrench control that cannot be contested or reversed.
  • Preventive surveillance with structured transparency Society and power
    Monitor everyone closely enough to stop misuse of a dangerous technology, using privacy-preserving methods so the data cannot easily be abused.
  • Spreading AI capability widely (diffusion) Society and power
    Spread AI capability and control across many people and groups, so no single actor holds most of the power.
  • Model welfare research AI welfare
    Investigate whether and when an AI system's welfare deserves moral consideration.
  • Taking AI welfare seriously AI welfare
    Argue a realistic possibility of consciousness or robust agency justifies acting now.
