FAR.AI

Research and education nonprofit that red-teams frontier models, studies deception and honesty training, runs AI safety events, hosts a Berkeley co-working space, and funds outside researchers.

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Hubs and forums for safety researchers Building the field
    Run the offices, campuses, conferences and online forums where safety researchers work side by side, meet and publish.
  • Reducing geographic constraints on AI-safety talent Building the field
    Widen the talent pool by expanding where AI-safety work gets done. People who cannot move to the Bay Area, London or Washington DC can then contribute through remote and part-time programmes, paid entry roles and local hubs.
  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work quickly through grantmakers and regrantors who know the field well enough to spot good projects.
  • Mild optimisation (satisficing) Theory
    Train systems to do "good enough" instead of maximising, cutting the pull toward extreme strategies.
  • Lie detection and honesty training Looking inside
    Build detectors that catch a model stating what it believes false, and train models to be honest rather than merely plausible.
  • Adversarial robustness and red-teaming Testing before release
    Attack the system deliberately to find and close jailbreaks.
  • Sandbagging and situational-awareness evaluations Testing before release
    Test whether a model hides capability or behaves differently when it knows it is being tested.
