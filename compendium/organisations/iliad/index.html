Iliad

Runs a month-long alignment course, a mentored fellowship and the ILIAD conference series, supports the Alignment Journal, and has research groups on debate, oversight and verification protocols.

Works on

  • Taking on unowned problems and agendas Building the field
    Take on safety problems and research agendas that no team owns, for example by founding a new organisation or lab.
  • Hubs and forums for safety researchers Building the field
    Run the offices, campuses, conferences and online forums where safety researchers work side by side, meet and publish.
  • Agent foundations Theory
    Formal theory of what an agent, a goal and rational choice even are.
  • Scalable oversight Data and training
    Let humans, helped by AI, supervise outputs too complex or costly to check by hand.
  • AI safety via debate Data and training
    Surface truth under complexity by making systems attack each other's answers.
