Truthful AI

Berkeley nonprofit led by Owain Evans studying deception, situational awareness and hidden reasoning in language models, including emergent misalignment.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Character and identity training Data and training
    Shape what the model thinks it is, on the theory that a stable self-concept generalises better than a rule list.
  • Model introspection Looking inside
    Study whether models can accurately report on their own internal states.
  • Sandbagging and situational-awareness evaluations Testing before release
    Test whether a model hides capability or behaves differently when it knows it is being tested.
  • Model organisms of misalignment Testing before release
    Build deliberately misaligned models in the lab to study how deception arises and test whether safety methods catch it.
