Evitable

Public-facing project, fiscally sponsored by the US nonprofit Anti Entropy and founded by AI professor David Krueger, that informs and organizes the public to stop the race to build superintelligence.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Public communication and content creation Building the field
    Explain AI risk to wide audiences through video, podcasts, books, social media and journalism.
  • Outside pressure on AI developers AI companies
    Use employees, public campaigns and organised civil society to push labs toward responsible behaviour.
  • Halting frontier AI development Global coordination
    Prepare the legal, technical and institutional means for an internationally coordinated halt to the most dangerous AI development.
