Frontier Model Forum

Industry-supported nonprofit of leading AI developers that publishes shared technical guidance on safety frameworks, risk thresholds, bio and cyber evaluations, and information sharing.

Works on

  • Think tank for AI labs Building the field
    On-demand independent expertise for lab safety and governance teams.
  • Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring Looking inside
    Read the model's written reasoning and check it reflects the real decision process.
  • Dangerous-capability evaluations Testing before release
    Test models for bio, cyber, persuasion, autonomy and scheming capabilities before release.
  • Adversarial robustness and red-teaming Testing before release
    Attack the system deliberately to find and close jailbreaks.
  • Independent third-party evaluation and audit AI companies
    Outside parties test and verify a lab's safety and capability claims.
  • Frontier-lab internal policy and safety culture AI companies
    Shape the labs' own risk management, red lines and incident reporting.
  • AI incident reporting systems and databases AI companies
    Collect reports of AI harms and near-harms in shared databases or required filings, so others learn and regulators can act.
