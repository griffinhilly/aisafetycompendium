Schmidt Sciences

Science philanthropy whose Safe AI programme funds basic technical research to understand, predict and control risks from frontier AI, with projects on multi-agent risk, interpretability and evaluation.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work quickly through grantmakers and regrantors who know the field well enough to spot good projects.
  • Multi-agent alignment and commitment dynamics Theory
    Prevent collusion, conflict or destabilising races between many AI systems.
  • Non-agentic and bounded AI designs Data and training
    Build a different kind of AI that is safe by design, such as a predictor with no goals of its own, instead of aligning goal-pursuing agents.
  • Mechanistic interpretability Looking inside
    Reverse-engineer the network's internals into human-readable circuits and algorithms.
  • Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring Looking inside
    Read the model's written reasoning and check it reflects the real decision process.
  • Model organisms of misalignment Testing before release
    Build deliberately misaligned models in the lab to study how deception arises and test whether safety methods catch it.
  • Low-probability estimation Testing before release
    Estimate how likely a model is to produce a rare catastrophic output, without having to find an input that triggers it.
  • Science of evaluations and capability elicitation Testing before release
    Make AI tests trustworthy: draw out a model's full ability, measure the right thing, and report statistical uncertainty.
  • Multi-agent evaluations and collusion detection Testing before release
    Test groups of interacting AI agents for collusion, hidden signalling and miscoordination, and build monitors that catch it.
