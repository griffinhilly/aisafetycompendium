Meaning Alignment Institute

Non-profit researching how to align AI, markets and democracies with what people value, including Democratic Fine-Tuning, which gathers public input to set a model's values.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Defining the alignment target What AI should value
    Sort the candidate things an AI could be aligned to — instructions, intentions, preferences, interests, values.
  • Alignment as fair treatment of claims What AI should value
    Pick principles by a fair process that can be justified publicly to people who disagree.
  • Public input into model values What AI should value
    Run deliberation and voting with members of the public to set the principles a model is trained to follow.
  • Empirical values research as input to normative debate What AI should value
    Study what values models actually express and what people actually want, to inform the "align to what" argument.
