Principles of Intelligence

Nonprofit that runs a roughly 3-month fellowship pairing researchers from other sciences with AI safety mentors, with fellowship tracks on gradual disempowerment, corrigibility and AI conflict.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Research training and upskilling programmes Building the field
    Run courses, bootcamps and mentored fellowships that train newcomers for AI safety research, engineering and policy work.
  • Shared offices, hubs, convenings and forums Building the field
    Run the offices, campuses, conferences and online forums where safety researchers work side by side, meet and publish.
  • Corrigibility as a formal target Theory
    Design systems that reliably accept correction and shutdown.
  • Multi-agent alignment and commitment dynamics Theory
    Prevent collusion, conflict or destabilising races between many AI systems.
  • Gradual-disempowerment research People's own judgement
    Study how humans lose meaningful control incrementally, with no single catastrophic event.
