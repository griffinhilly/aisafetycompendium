Survival and Flourishing Fund

Virtual fund that runs the S-Process, a grant-recommendation process; recent rounds support AI safety and governance organisations, and the 2026 round includes a Human Self-Enhancement and Empowerment theme.

Works on

  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work fast through people with taste and dealflow.
  • Public communication and content creation Building the field
    Explain AI risk to wide audiences through video, podcasts, books, social media and journalism.
  • Agent foundations Theory
    Formal theory of what an agent, a goal and rational choice even are.
  • Human empowerment as an explicit design goal People's own judgement
    Build AI that augments rather than substitutes for human capability, on purpose.
  • Human self-enhancement and empowerment People's own judgement
    Raise human capability directly, so humans can keep pace with AI rather than be replaced by it.
  • Agreement verification technology Global coordination
    Build the technology and protocols that let governments, or AI developers, verify that an AI agreement is being kept.
