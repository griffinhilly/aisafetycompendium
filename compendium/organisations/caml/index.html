Compassion Aligned Machine Learning

California nonprofit researching how to align AI with the welfare of animals and other sentient beings, through benchmarks (CompassionBench, TAC) and training-data work.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Including animals in alignment targets What AI should value
    Make animal interests something AI is aligned to weigh.
  • Data filtering, synthetic data, poisoning defence, hyperstition studies Data and training
    Control what goes into training so hazardous behaviour never gets learned.
  • Multi-axis value and virtue benchmarking Testing before release
    Score models on many value dimensions at once and compare their profiles.
