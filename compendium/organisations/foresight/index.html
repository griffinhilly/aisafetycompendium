Foresight Institute

Funds AI safety work through grants in four areas: automating research, security technologies, safe multi-agent scenarios and neurotechnology.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work fast through people with taste and dealflow.
  • Automated safety research Building the field
    Uses AI systems to do alignment research, so safety work can keep pace with growing capabilities.
  • Human self-enhancement and empowerment People's own judgement
    Raise human capability directly, so humans can keep pace with AI rather than be replaced by it.
  • Defence acceleration (d/acc) Society and power
    Deliberately speed up defensive technology faster than offensive, across physical, bio, cyber and information domains, plus social technology.
  • Spreading AI capability widely (diffusion) Society and power
    Spread AI capability and control across many people and groups, so no single actor holds most of the power.
