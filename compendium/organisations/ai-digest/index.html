AI Digest (project of Sage)

Project that runs the AI Village, where several AI agents pursue goals together online, and publishes what they do and where they go wrong.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Public communication and content creation Building the field
    Explain AI risk to wide audiences through video, podcasts, books, social media and journalism.
  • Multi-agent evaluations and collusion detection Testing before release
    Test groups of interacting AI agents for collusion, hidden signalling and miscoordination, and build monitors that catch it.
  • Real-world trials of AI agents Testing before release
    Run AI agents with real money, tools and people in open-ended settings, and record how they behave and fail.
