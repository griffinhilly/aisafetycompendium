  • National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA)United StatesSemi-autonomous agency in the US Department of Energy; since April 2024 it has assessed Anthropic's models for nuclear proliferation risk and co-built a classifier (announced 21 August 2025) that flags concerning nuclear conversations.
  • Organisation for Economic Co-operation and DevelopmentFranceOperates the OECD.AI AI Incidents and Hazards Monitor, which documents AI incidents and hazards from news sources so policymakers can see risk patterns.
  • Singapore AI Safety Institute (Digital Trust Centre)SingaporeSingapore's AI Safety Institute, hosted at NTU's Digital Trust Centre with IMDA; builds AI evaluation tools, runs red-teaming challenges and joint testing with other institutes.

  • US Commerce Department, Bureau of Industry and SecurityUnited StatesProposed on 29 January 2024 that US cloud providers verify foreign customers and report foreign training of large AI models; also writes chip and model-weight export controls.
  • US Federal Trade CommissionUnited StatesApplies competition and consumer-protection law to AI; its January 2025 staff report examined cloud-provider investments in AI developers and the risk of lock-in.
