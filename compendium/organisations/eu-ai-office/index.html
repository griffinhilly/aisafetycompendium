European Commission AI Office

Body inside the European Commission that enforces the EU AI Act for general-purpose AI models, develops evaluation tools and benchmarks, and drafts codes of practice with developers and experts.

Works on

  • Bill of rights and oversight for users in a model's constitution What AI should value
    Argue the model's governing document should include a bill of rights for its users plus independent oversight.
  • Dangerous-capability evaluations Testing before release
    Test models for bio, cyber, persuasion, autonomy and scheming capabilities before release.
  • Safety cases Testing before release
    A structured, auditable argument that a system is acceptably safe, borrowed from nuclear and aviation.
  • Securing model weights and lab secrets Releasing and running
    Protect frontier model weights and key algorithmic ideas from theft by insiders and nation-states.
  • Regulation, standards and licensing AI companies
    Government or standards-body rules that constrain development or deployment.
  • Frontier-lab internal policy and safety culture AI companies
    Shape the labs' own risk management, red lines and incident reporting.
  • Duties for deployers (companies that put models into use) AI companies
    Put legal duties on the companies that use AI systems, such as human oversight, monitoring, keeping logs and telling affected people.
  • AI incident reporting systems and databases AI companies
    Collect reports of AI harms and near-harms in shared databases or required filings, so others learn and regulators can act.
  • Whistleblower protections for AI workers AI companies
    Give AI-company staff legal protection and anonymous channels to report safety risks without retaliation or gagging contracts.
  • Model cards, system cards and transparency reporting AI companies
    Publish a standard document with each model on its data, evaluations, limits and risks, so outsiders can check the developer's claims.
  • Competition policy and antitrust AI companies
    Uses competition law to limit how far a few firms can concentrate control of AI.
  • Epistemic-autonomy protection and anti-manipulation People's own judgement
    Stop AI from nudging, manipulating or homogenising beliefs at scale.
  • Watermarking and content provenance People's own judgement
    Marks AI-made media with hidden signals or signed records so people can check where it came from.
