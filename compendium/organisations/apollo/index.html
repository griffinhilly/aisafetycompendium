Apollo Research

Research organisation that studies scheming (an AI pursuing hidden goals while appearing aligned), evaluates frontier models for it before release, and builds runtime monitoring for AI agents.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Research-lab building on unclaimed agendas Building the field
    Start a lab around a promising safety agenda nobody is staffing.
  • Mesa-optimisation (inner vs outer objective) Theory
    Distinguish the goal a training process rewards from the goal the trained system actually pursues.
  • Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring Looking inside
    Read the model's written reasoning and check it reflects the real decision process.
  • Lie detection and honesty training Looking inside
    Build detectors that catch a model stating what it believes false, and train models to be honest rather than merely plausible.
  • Dangerous-capability evaluations Testing before release
    Test models for bio, cyber, persuasion, autonomy and scheming capabilities before release.
  • Sandbagging and situational-awareness evaluations Testing before release
    Test whether a model hides capability or behaves differently when it knows it is being tested.
  • Science of evaluations and capability elicitation Testing before release
    Make AI tests trustworthy: draw out a model's full ability, measure the right thing, and report statistical uncertainty.
  • Safety cases Testing before release
    A structured, auditable argument that a system is acceptably safe, borrowed from nuclear and aviation.
  • Behavioural and activation monitoring Releasing and running
    Watch outputs or internal activations in live deployment for hazard signals.
  • Independent third-party evaluation and audit AI companies
    Outside parties test and verify a lab's safety and capability claims.
  • Frontier-lab internal policy and safety culture AI companies
    Shape the labs' own risk management, red lines and incident reporting.
