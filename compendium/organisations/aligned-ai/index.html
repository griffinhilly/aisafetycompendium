Aligned AI

Oxford-based alignment company; published work on goal misgeneralisation (CoinRun, 2023) and on defending models against jailbreaks (February 2025).

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Robustness and goal generalisation under distribution shift Data and training
    Make a model keep both its skills and its intended goals when deployment conditions differ from training.
  • Adversarial robustness and red-teaming Testing before release
    Attack the system deliberately to find and close jailbreaks.
  • Input and output classifiers (guardrails) Releasing and running
    Run separate classifier models on users' prompts and the model's responses, and block harmful ones in real time.
