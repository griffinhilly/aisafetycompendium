Advanced Research + Invention Agency (ARIA)

UK government R&D funding agency. Its Safeguarded AI programme funds formal-methods tools to give AI systems provable safety guarantees, and Scaling Trust funds secure coordination between AI agents.

Works on

  • Law-following AI What AI should value
    Give AI agents actual legal duties, so they refuse illegal acts on a principal's behalf.
  • Guaranteed-safe AI and formal verification Theory
    Build systems with mathematical proofs of safe behaviour, not just tests.
  • Multi-agent alignment and commitment dynamics Theory
    Prevent collusion, conflict or destabilising races between many AI systems.
  • Multi-agent evaluations and collusion detection Testing before release
    Test groups of interacting AI agents for collusion, hidden signalling and miscoordination, and build monitors that catch it.
  • Hardening society's defences Society and power
    Make society harder to harm with AI-enabled attacks, by building defences in the areas AI most helps attackers, above all computer security and biological threats, using AI tools among other means.
