Scale AI (Scale Labs)

AI data company whose Scale Labs publishes benchmarks including Fortress, an evaluation of frontier-model risks to national security and public safety.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Unlearning hazardous knowledge Data and training
    Remove dangerous knowledge, such as bioweapon or cyberattack know-how, from a trained model while keeping its general skills.
  • Circuit breakers and representation rerouting Data and training
    Train the model so the internal patterns that lead to harmful output get redirected, which cuts the output off. The authors say this works even against attacks it was not trained on.
  • Latent adversarial training Data and training
    During training, attack the model's internal activations rather than only its inputs, so it resists failures nobody has found yet.
  • Lie detection and honesty training Looking inside
    Build detectors that catch a model stating what it believes false, and train models to be honest rather than merely plausible.
  • Dangerous-capability evaluations Testing before release
    Test models for bio, cyber, persuasion, autonomy and scheming capabilities before release.
