Krueger AI Safety Lab

Academic AI safety research group led by David Krueger, working on alignment, gradual disempowerment, manipulation, goal misgeneralisation and technical AI governance.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Mesa-optimisation (inner vs outer objective) Theory
    Distinguish the goal a training process rewards from the goal the trained system actually pursues.
  • Robustness and goal generalisation under distribution shift Data and training
    Make a model keep both its skills and its intended goals when deployment conditions differ from training.
  • Manipulation characterisation People's own judgement
    Define and measure manipulation as distinct from legitimate persuasion.
  • Gradual disempowerment People's own judgement
    Study how humans could lose meaningful control step by step, with no single catastrophic event. One method simulates many small choices to rely on AI.
