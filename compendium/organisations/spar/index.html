SPAR (Supervised Program for Alignment Research)

Part-time remote research fellowship pairing aspiring researchers with professionals on AI safety, policy and security projects, ending in a Demo Day.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Sandbagging and situational-awareness evaluations Testing before release
    Test whether a model hides capability or behaves differently when it knows it is being tested.
  • Autonomy-preserving benchmarks Testing before release
    Directly test whether a model respects the user's agency rather than fostering dependency.
  • Model-character evaluation against a written spec Testing before release
    Measure how closely a model's behaviour matches its developer's own specification.
  • AI control (restrict and monitor an untrusted model) Releasing and running
    Design deployment so a misaligned model still cannot cause harm.
  • Agent-based modelling of agency loss People's own judgement
    Simulate disempowerment as accumulated small surrenders rather than a takeover event.
  • Concentration-of-power and lock-in research Society and power
    Study how AI lets a small group entrench control that cannot be contested or reversed.
