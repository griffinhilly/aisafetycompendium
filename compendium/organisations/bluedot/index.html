BlueDot Impact

Non-profit that runs courses, grants and events to move people into AI safety careers, including a 12-unit AI Alignment course.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Founding new organisations on unowned problems Building the field
    Fund people to start the organisations the field lacks.
  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work fast through people with taste and dealflow.
  • Career advice and job matching Building the field
    Give people free career research, one-on-one advice and curated job listings that steer them into AI safety roles.
  • Research training and upskilling programmes Building the field
    Run courses, bootcamps and mentored fellowships that train newcomers for AI safety research, engineering and policy work.
  • Reducing geographic constraints on AI-safety talent Building the field
    Widen the talent pool by expanding where AI-safety work gets done, so people who cannot move to the Bay Area, London or Washington DC can still contribute through remote and part-time programmes, paid entry roles and local hubs.
