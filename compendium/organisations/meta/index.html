Meta (AI safety: Llama Guard and Purple Llama)

Technology company that releases the Llama models and open safety tools: Llama Guard input and output classifiers and CyberSec Eval cybersecurity benchmarks, grouped as Purple Llama.

Works on

  • Public input into model values What AI should value
    Run deliberation and voting with members of the public to set the principles a model is trained to follow.
  • Latent adversarial training Data and training
    During training, attack the model's internal activations rather than only its inputs, so it resists failures nobody has found yet.
  • Dangerous-capability evaluations Testing before release
    Test models for bio, cyber, persuasion, autonomy and scheming capabilities before release.
  • Safety cases Testing before release
    A structured, auditable argument that a system is acceptably safe, borrowed from nuclear and aviation.
  • Input and output classifiers (guardrails) Releasing and running
    Run separate classifier models on users' prompts and the model's responses, and block harmful ones in real time.
  • Frontier-lab internal policy and safety culture AI companies
    Shape the labs' own risk management, red lines and incident reporting.
  • Spreading AI capability widely (diffusion) Society and power
    Spread AI capability and control across many people and groups, so no single actor holds most of the power.
