Center for Human-Compatible AI

UC Berkeley research lab that aims to re-found AI on provably beneficial principles, with AI systems that stay uncertain about human objectives and defer to people.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Assistance games / cooperative inverse reinforcement learning Theory
    Model the AI as uncertain about what the human wants, so it defers rather than assumes.
  • Corrigibility as a formal target Theory
    Design systems that reliably accept correction and shutdown.
