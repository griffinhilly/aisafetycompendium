Alignment Research Center

Nonprofit doing theoretical research toward explaining and predicting neural network behaviour from their internals, including estimating rare bad outputs and the eliciting latent knowledge problem.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Get involved or get in touch

Works on

  • Eliciting latent knowledge (ELK) Looking inside
    Design objectives that make a model honestly report what it internally believes.
  • Low-probability estimation Testing before release
    Estimate how likely a model is to produce a rare catastrophic output, without having to find an input that triggers it.
  • Anomaly detection Releasing and running
    Flag statistically unusual behaviour as a proxy for an unknown hazard.

People linked to it

  • Ajeya Cotra: AI-safety researcher known for the "Biological Anchors" report on AI timelines and for widely-cited writing on AI takeover risk; now on technical staff at METR after years directing AI-safety grantmaking at Open Philanthropy.
  • Paul Christiano: Alignment researcher; first author of the 2017 RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback — training a model using human ratings of its outputs as the reward signal) paper, and one of the most-cited technical thinkers in AI alignment.
