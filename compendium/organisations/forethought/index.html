Forethought

Research nonprofit in Oxford studying how to navigate the transition to superintelligent AI, including power concentration, lock-in, AI for decision-making, and space governance.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • AI philosophical competence (metaphilosophy) What AI should value
    Make AI systems better at reasoning about hard philosophical questions, so they help rather than mislead on them.
  • Avoiding premature lock-in (the long reflection) What AI should value
    Keep key choices reversible, and avoid irreversible steps until humanity has had time to work out what it wants.
  • Hard constraints against concentration of power What AI should value
    Forbid the model outright from helping any individual or group seize unprecedented and illegitimate degrees of absolute control.
  • Planned handover of AGI control AI companies
    A lab commits in advance to hand control of an aligned AGI to a more democratically legitimate body.
  • National AI project AI companies
    Run frontier AI development as a government-led effort, for security against espionage and safety management.
  • Keeping a democratic lead in AI Global coordination
    Give advanced AI to the intelligence and defence communities of the US and its democratic allies; restrict chips.
  • Space resource governance Global coordination
    Agree rules now against exclusive grabs of offworld resources, before advanced AI makes them reachable.
  • Differential technology development Society and power
    Delay risky innovation and accelerate safer substitutes, adjusting relative timing.
  • Improved epistemics and decision quality Society and power
    Strengthen forecasting and institutional decision-making as a general safety lever.
  • Concentration-of-power and lock-in research Society and power
    Study how AI lets a small group entrench control that cannot be contested or reversed.
  • Spreading AI capability widely (diffusion) Society and power
    Spread AI capability and control across many people and groups, so no single actor holds most of the power.
  • Deals and negotiation with AI systems AI welfare
    Make agreements with AI systems so that cooperating pays them better than defecting.
