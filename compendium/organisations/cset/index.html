Center for Security and Emerging Technology

Georgetown University policy research centre analysing the security implications of AI; its work includes AI red-teaming practice, AI incident analysis and AI chip tracking.

Works on

  • Adversarial robustness and red-teaming Testing before release
    Attack the system deliberately to find and close jailbreaks.
  • Compute governance AI companies
    Govern hardware and compute inputs as a lever on who can train frontier systems.
  • AI incident reporting systems and databases AI companies
    Collect reports of AI harms and near-harms in shared databases or required filings, so others learn and regulators can act.
  • Offence–defence balance research Society and power
    Model how AI shifts the relative cost of attack versus defence in a domain.
  • Societal readiness mitigations Society and power
    Use AI systems to harden society's defences against AI-enabled risks in advance, for example by rapidly patching vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure or detecting pandemics early.
