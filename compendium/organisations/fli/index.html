Future of Life Institute

Nonprofit working to reduce large-scale risks from AI, biotechnology and nuclear weapons, through policy work, grantmaking, public campaigns and a company safety index.

Works on

  • Grantmaking and regranting Building the field
    Move money to safety work fast through people with taste and dealflow.
  • Public communication and content creation Building the field
    Explain AI risk to wide audiences through video, podcasts, books, social media and journalism.
  • Safer alternative AI designs (non-agentic and bounded systems) Data and training
    Build a different kind of AI that is safe by design, such as a predictor with no goals of its own, instead of aligning goal-pursuing agents.
  • Independent third-party evaluation and audit AI companies
    Outside parties test and verify a lab's safety and capability claims.
  • Liability and tort mechanisms AI companies
    Make developers legally bear the cost of catastrophic AI harm.
  • Regulation, standards and licensing AI companies
    Government or standards-body rules that constrain development or deployment.
  • Frontier-lab internal policy and safety culture AI companies
    Shape the labs' own risk management, red lines and incident reporting.
  • Outside pressure on AI developers AI companies
    Use employees, public campaigns and organised civil society to push labs toward responsible behaviour.
  • Planned handover of AGI control AI companies
    A lab commits in advance to hand control of an aligned AGI to a more democratically legitimate body.
  • Halting frontier AI development Global coordination
    Prepare the legal, technical and institutional means for an internationally coordinated halt to the most dangerous AI development.
  • International regulatory and inspection agency (IAEA-style) Global coordination
    An international body that sets AI standards and checks that states and developers follow them.
  • International joint safety lab (CERN-style) Global coordination
    Countries pool people and computing in one shared institution to do frontier AI or AI safety research.
  • Oversight and arms control of military AI Global coordination
    Keep humans in control of AI used in military command, control and decision support, through agreements and national policy.
  • Predistribution and windfall mechanisms Society and power
    Decide who holds equity, access and governance rights before the gains arrive, not after.
