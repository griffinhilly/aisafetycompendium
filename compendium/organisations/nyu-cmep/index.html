NYU Center for Mind, Ethics, and Policy

Research center at New York University on the sentience and moral status of nonhuman minds, including AI systems; co-authored the 2024 report Taking AI Welfare Seriously.

Works on

  • Grounds of moral status AI welfare
    Argue what capacity earns a being moral consideration — sentience, cognitive sophistication, or something else.
  • Model welfare research AI welfare
    Investigate whether and when an AI system's welfare deserves moral consideration.
  • Taking AI welfare seriously AI welfare
    Argue a realistic possibility of consciousness or robust agency justifies acting now.
  • Otherness and control AI welfare
    Ask how agents with different values should relate, seeing some AIs as fellow creatures, not mere tools.
  • AI as an object of moral-circle expansion AI welfare
    Study empirically who extends moral concern to AI and why.
  • Legal personhood and standing for AI AI welfare
    Give some AI systems a legal status, such as the right to make contracts, hold property or sue.
