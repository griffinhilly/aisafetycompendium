Alibaba Cloud (Qwen team)

Qwen team at Alibaba Cloud released Qwen3Guard, open safety moderation models that classify prompts and responses, including token by token during streaming.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Process supervision (reward reasoning steps, not outcomes) Data and training
    Reward each step of a model's reasoning rather than only its final answer, so training favours sound, checkable reasoning.
  • Input and output classifiers (guardrails) Releasing and running
    Run separate classifier models on users' prompts and the model's responses, and block harmful ones in real time.
