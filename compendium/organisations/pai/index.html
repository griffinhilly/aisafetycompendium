Partnership on AI

Nonprofit partnership of industry, academia, civil society and media that publishes guidance on safe foundation-model deployment and supported the AI Incident Database.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Impact measures: relative reachability, attainable utility preservation Theory
    Penalise the AI for making large or irreversible changes to the world state.
  • Frontier-lab internal policy and safety culture AI companies
    Shape the labs' own risk management, red lines and incident reporting.
  • Dual-use research review AI companies
    Funders and institutions screen research for misuse risk before they fund or allow it.
  • AI incident reporting systems and databases AI companies
    Collect reports of AI harms and near-harms in shared databases or required filings, so others learn and regulators can act.
  • Proof of personhood (personhood credentials) People's own judgement
    Lets a person prove an online account belongs to a real human without revealing who they are.
