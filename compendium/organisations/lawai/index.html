Institute for Law & AI

Think tank doing legal research on AI, including how to make AI agents follow the law and how to structure international agreements on frontier AI.

Visit websiteOpen in the compendium

Works on

  • Law-following AI What AI should value
    Give AI agents actual legal duties, so they refuse illegal acts on a principal's behalf.
  • Liability and tort mechanisms AI companies
    Make developers legally bear the cost of catastrophic AI harm.
  • Catastrophe bonds and risk bonds AI companies
    Sell tradeable securities that lose principal if a defined AI disaster occurs, so the market prices the risk.
  • Independent AI incident investigation (NTSB-style) AI companies
    Outside investigators review real AI incidents after the fact and publish lessons, as air-crash boards do.
  • Benefit-sharing for joining governance (Atoms for Peace model) Global coordination
    Offers states access to AI benefits in return for joining safety governance, as Atoms for Peace did for nuclear technology.
