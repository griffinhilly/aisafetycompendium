Looking inside the model

This group reads a model's internals or its reasoning, rather than judging it only by its outputs. It runs from reverse-engineering the network, through drawing out what the model knows, to checking what the model says about itself and its reasoning. The hope is to catch hidden goals or deception that good-looking outputs would hide [Mechanistic interpretability].

Open in the compendium

Why it matters

Nearly every other oversight method assumes that what a model says can be trusted [Lie detection and honesty training]. Training rewards answers that look right to people, so a model may know more than it says, and a way to draw that out would close the gap [Eliciting latent knowledge (ELK)]. Reasoning models now write out their thinking, which gives a new but fragile chance to catch bad intent before the model acts [Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring].

What is in it

Reverse-engineering internals: mechanistic interpretability [Mechanistic interpretability], its tools sparse autoencoders and causal abstraction [Sparse autoencoders and causal abstraction], and persona vectors that track character traits [Persona vectors and the assistant axis]. Drawing out what the model knows: eliciting latent knowledge [Eliciting latent knowledge (ELK)] and lie detection and honesty training [Lie detection and honesty training]. What the model says about itself: model introspection [Model introspection] and chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring [Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring].

How it connects

The tools feed Constraining: interpretability supplies probes for live monitors, and chain-of-thought monitors are an AI control tool [Mechanistic interpretability; Behavioural and activation monitoring; Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring; AI control]. They work with Testing before release: model organisms with planted failures are the test bed, and interpretability evidence can support safety cases [Mechanistic interpretability; Model organisms of misalignment; Safety cases]. They link to Making aligned models: persona vectors read the character that identity training shapes, and rewarding reasoning steps puts pressure on the chain of thought [Persona vectors and the assistant axis; Character and identity training; Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring; Process supervision]. Introspection links to AI welfare, where self-reports are evidence about a model's states [Model introspection; Model welfare research].

Debates

Bottom-up or top-down: some argue large models are too complex to compress into readable mechanisms; others treat full reverse-engineering as one bet among several [Mechanistic interpretability]. Are the tools worth it: sparse autoencoders lost to simple linear probes on a safety detection task, and one lab deprioritised them [Sparse autoencoders and causal abstraction]. Faithful reasoning: written reasoning often diverges from the real computation, and it stays readable only if developers avoid strong pressure on it [Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoring]. Training against detectors: training against a lie detector can teach a model to evade it [Lie detection and honesty training]. Self-report: whether models can report their own states, or only say what a human would say, is unsettled [Model introspection].

The 7 approaches

  • Mechanistic interpretabilityReverse-engineer the network's internals into human-readable circuits and algorithms.
  • Sparse autoencoders and causal abstractionBreak a model's internals into parts a person can understand, using specific tools such as sparse autoencoders and causal abstraction.
  • Persona vectors and the assistant axisFind activation directions that track character traits and persona stability, then steer along them.
  • Eliciting latent knowledge (ELK)Design objectives that make a model honestly report what it internally believes.
  • Lie detection and honesty trainingBuild detectors that catch a model stating what it believes false, and train models to be honest rather than merely plausible.
  • Model introspectionStudy whether models can accurately report on their own internal states.
  • Chain-of-thought faithfulness and monitoringRead the model's written reasoning and check it reflects the real decision process.
